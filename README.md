## Individual variability in hypertrophic responses to resistance exercise training.
Progressive resistance exercise training(RT) is a type of exercise training where the skeletal muscles are contracted against progressively increased external weights. RT stimulates physiological adaptations such as increases in bone mineral density, muscle sctrength and to muscle hypertrophy.  Current RT protocols adopt a one-size-fits-all approach despite the well-documented heterogeneity of individual responses. This study combines machine learning and explainable artificial intelligence to identify genes related to hypertrophic responses to RT.
The analysis is based on data from five different RT intervention studies 
* Alpha and Omega [ClinicalTrials.gov ID NCT04279951](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04279951)
* ContraTRAIN [ClinicalTrials.gov ID NCT03795025](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03795025)
* COPD [ClinicalTrials.gov ID NCT02598830](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02598830) Results published at the [Journal of Translational Medicine](https://translational-medicine.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12967-021-02969-1) and [J cachexia, sarcopenia and Muscle](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jcsm.12688)
* ReLief [ClinicalTrials.gov ID NCT05063279](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05063279)
* Volume [ClinicalTrials.gov ID NCT03795025](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03795025) Results published at [BMC Bioinformatics](https://bmcbioinformatics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12859-022-04791-y)
